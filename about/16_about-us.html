2019 – Guinness World Record
For us, protection is more than insurance. That is why we launched a campaign to educate the consumers about the importance of organ donation. Our efforts led us to set a Guinness World record for securing the maximum number of organ donation pledges in 24 hours.

The Shining Moments that Contributed to Our Success

2018 - The Revolutionary Better Half Benefit
Continuing our journey of product innovation & customer centricity led us to creating Edelweiss Life Zindagi Plus in August 2018. The highlight of this product was the introduction of an industry-first revolutionary feature – the Better Half Benefit. With this, consumers could get a life cover for their spouse for increased protection for the family.

The Shining Moments that Contributed to Our Success

2017 - Keep Innovating, Keep Rising
Our dedication to design new products to suit the evolving needs of our customers led us to disrupt the industry by creating Edelweiss Life Wealth Ultima - our new age low cost ULIP. We were awarded the Best Product Innovation Award at the Indian Insurance Awards for our efforts and drive.

2014 - Approaching New Horizons
We launched the first online term plan - Edelweiss Life My Life+, which offered a long life cover of 80 years. It was designed to meet the needs and convenience of our new age customers and bring them the ease of online insurance purchase.

The Shining Moments that Contributed to Our Success

It’s been a wonderful journey so far, made better by these moments!

2013 - Gaining Laurels and Recognition
We gained recognition for our efforts at Indian Insurance Awards, where we were bestowed the Rising Star Insurer Award. This honor is given to acknowledge a stand-out young insurer, already showing signs of becoming a major force to reckon with, in the years ahead.

The Shining Moments that Contributed to Our Success

It’s been a wonderful journey so far, made better by these moments!

2012 - Establishing The Path To Victory
Built on the belief ‘Insurance se badhkar hai aapki zaroorat’, Vijaypath made a splash upon introduction. Due to the mis-selling in the industry back then, this industry-first approach was a welcome launch as it showed consumers that we spend more time doing need analysis to suggest products that match their needs.

A joint venture between Edelweiss Financial Services Limited - one of India’s leading and diversified financial services companies and Tokio Marine Holdings Inc - one of the oldest (around 140 years) and largest insurance companies in Japan, led to the beginning of Edelweiss Life Insurance Company Limited.

Our Mantra for Achieving Constant Success

Our Mantra for Achieving Constant Success

Everyone has dreams and aspirations for their future that they want to achieve. Here at Edelweiss Life Insurance Company Limited, our mission is to make these goals a reality. Our expertise and resources are constantly at work, to identify potential hindrances and devise solutions to counter them.

Quality

Our main aim is to provide quality above all else. This extends to all spheres of our service - quality of our interaction, quality of our advice, quality of our products and quality of our post-purchase service.

Cost Consciousness

We believe everyone should be able to get a safety net to protect their future. That is why we put our efforts into managing our operations and our people in a cost-effective manner.

Customer Centric

We wish to bring effective solutions for our customers. Thus, we always look at every product, every service point and every innovation through our customer’s perspective. We believe this reflects in our advisory process, products, customer service and claims process.

    Sumit Rai

    MD & CEO

    Sumit Rai
    MD & CEO

    Sumit Rai is currently the MD & CEO of Edelweiss Life Insurance. Setting the strategic direction for the company, Sumit has nurtured a strong leadership team to build a multi-channel distribution network and a robust portfolio of offerings for its customers. A charismatic leader with an experience spanning over two and a half decades, he has mentored several cross-functional teams to enhance the organizational value and growth during his tenure so far.

    Under Sumit’s leadership, Edelweiss Life has earned several industry recognitions, bolstered by the key mantras of customer centricity and innovation. Driven by his consistent belief in delivering value for every aspect of the business, the company has achieved rapid business growth, introduced exciting products and service innovations, and truly delivered on its promise of making #ZindagiUnlimited for its customers and employees. Before taking charge as the MD & CEO, Sumit served as the company’s Chief Distribution Officer for a brief period.

    Previously, as a founder team member at Max Life, Sumit played a key role with the team that created one of India’s largest life insurance companies and handled multiple roles in distribution and strategy, including running and strengthening India’s largest non-captive, commercial bancassurance partnership.

    He was also associated with Bank of America, and ABN Amro Bank in the past, wherein he was instrumental in strategizing and implementing various strategic initiatives for the respective companies. 

    Subhrajit Mukhopadhyay

    Deputy CEO & Executive Director

    Subhrajit Mukhopadhyay
    Deputy CEO & Executive Director

    Subhrajit Mukhopadhyay is Deputy CEO & Executive Director of Edelweiss Life Insurance. With over 25 years of experience, he brings strategic foresight, innovation, and operational excellence to his leadership. A qualified actuary and Fellow of Institute of Actuaries of India, he has played a pivotal role in shaping the company’s growth trajectory and positioning it as a future-ready, customer-focused company.

     

    With a sharp focus on innovation, evolving customer behaviour and risk management, Subhrajit has helped lay a strong foundation for sustainable growth. His leadership has brought strategic clarity, enabling Edelweiss Life to navigate market complexities while strengthening its core. Under his guidance, the company has led with industry-first innovations, delivered top-tier fund performance, enhanced capital efficiency, and fortified its risk management practices—creating long-term value for customers and stakeholders.

     

    An industry veteran, he began his career with LIC and has previously held leadership roles at Exide Life Insurance before joining Edelweiss Life Insurance.

    Saba Adil

    Chief Human Resource Officer

    Saba Adil
    Chief Human Resource Officer

    Saba Adil is currently the Chief Human Resource Officer of Edelweiss Life Insurance.
     
    As the main force behind driving the People strategy, Saba plays an instrumental role in driving the company’s Culture, Purpose, and Employee Value Proposition to support its long-term vision. Saba brings with her 24+ years of experience in HR and has handled additional business roles in Operations, Customer Service, Renewals & Persistency, Risk, and Marketing, providing her a holistic understanding of conceptualizing and implementing effective people interventions. Her expertise lies in building organizations through robust people practices, cultivating capabilities and culture to achieve organizational objectives, and creating an engaging workplace for our employees. 
     
    She has previously held key positions at Raheja QBE General Insurance, Aegon Life Insurance, Avanse Financial Services and MGL.
     
    Saba holds a certificate in Corporate Innovation from the Stanford Graduate School of Business Executive Education and has a Master’s in Human Resources from AMU.

    Kayzad Hiramanek

    Chief Operating Officer

    Kayzad Hiramanek
    Chief Operating Officer

    Kayzad Hiramanek is currently the Chief Operating Officer of Edelweiss Life Insurance.

    He is a seasoned professional who brings 2.5 decades of experience in managing operations, technology, and customer experience strategy for businesses across sectors. At Edelweiss Life, he leads and executes the company’s Operations, Technology and Business Excellence efforts to elevate scalability, agility, and cost efficiencies within the business. Under his functional stewardship, the company aspires to build operational resilience and sustainability to weather through business cycles and any unprecedented events.

    In his previous experience, Kayzad has played an instrumental role in defining the Operations and Customer Experience strategy for organizations like Bajaj Allianz Life Insurance, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, and Avantha Ergo Life Insurance. He has also been associated with Bharti Airtel, Idea Cellular, Taj Hotels and more in the past.
