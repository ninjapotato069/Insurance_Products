Who we are

IndusInd Nippon Life Insurance is a leading private life insurance company in India, committed to helping individuals and families plan, protect, and build their financial future. The company brings together the legacy of IndusInd International Holdings Ltd. (IIHL) inspired by the enduring strength and prosperity of the Indus Valley civilization, and Nippon, representing global excellence, discipline, and trust. Together, we combine Indian values with international standards of service and governance

What we offer

We have a comprehensive and competitive range of protection, savings, retirement, and investment solutions, designed to meet diverse financial goals across life stages, from income protection and child education to wealth creation and retirement planning. Our products are built on the principles of transparency, value, and long-term assurance.

What we stand for

Our customers, our partners and our workforce are at the heart of everything we do. We are driven by a single purpose – to be a trusted partner who empowers every individual to be ready for every stage of life.

We stand on the strong pillars of customer centricity, innovation, and simplification, ensuring that life insurance is easy to buy, easy to understand, and easy to service. We are proud to be recognized as a ‘Great Place to Work’, fostering a culture that encourages performance, learning, and inclusivity.

Our Vision

Planning people's future and standing by them in their hour of need goes beyond business, it takes a selfless intent of thinking well for others. Our advisors enjoy high credibility and stature in society, having helped not only shape the future of families, but also recuperate in tough times. 'Do Good' is our intent, our philosophy and belief that we aim to bring alive through every life we touch.

To be a company people are proud of, trust in, and grow with providing financial independence to every life we touch
