Core Values

At CALI, we are committed to doing the right things for (a) our Partners (b) our Customers and (c) our Employees. Towards this end objective, our values include a customer-first approach, dedication to transparency and the integrity to deliver on promises. Our company culture and ethos are built on these foundational principles. We also focus on revolutionizing the microinsurance landscape through technological and product innovation without ever losing the inherent simplicity required to enable the uninsured to change their lives.
Effective solutions with Significant Impact – towards this objective, Our Values and Culture are the pillars behind it.

BEWARE OF SPURIOUS PHONE CALLS AND FICTITIOUS/FRAUDULENT OFFERS

IRDAI or any of its officials do not involve in any activities of Insurance business like selling insurance policies, announcing bonus or investment of premiums, refund of amounts, Policyholders or the prospects receiving such phone calls are requested to lodge a police complaint.
