What is Life Insurance?

Life Insurance is a financial product that provides a payout to beneficiaries in the event of the policyholder's unfortunate demise. It offers financial protection and security for your loved ones, helping to cover expenses such as debts, living costs, and other financial needs. It acts as a safety net, ensuring your family’s future goals stay on track even in your absence. Beyond protection, certain plans also help you build savings or wealth over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about life insurance, life insurance plans, policies, coverage, and more.

1.

What is the best life insurance policy in India?

The best life insurance policy in India depends on your goals. For maximum life cover at the lowest cost, a term life insurance plan is the best choice. For savings with life cover, an endowment or savings life insurance plan is ideal. For investment with life cover, a ULIP life insurance plan works best. Shriram Life's range of life insurance policies is designed to offer the best life insurance cover for every need and budget.

2.

How to buy life insurance online?

You can buy life insurance online with Shriram Life in 3 simple steps:

  1. Choose your life insurance plan on our website.
  2. Enter your details and get an instant life insurance quote.
  3. Pay online and receive your life insurance policy document by email.

Buying an online life insurance policy with us is 100% paperless and can be completed without a branch visit or agent assistance (Please read the policy document, benefits and exclusions carefully before you buy).

3.

How much life insurance coverage do I need?

Financial experts recommend life insurance coverage of at least 10–15 times your annual income. For example, if you earn ₹6 lakh per year, you should have at least ₹60–90 lakh in life insurance coverage. Your life insurance cover should also account for outstanding loans, your children's education costs, and your family's living expenses. Use our free Life Insurance Calculator on this page to find the right coverage amount.

4.

Which is the best life insurance company in India?

The best life insurance company in India should have a high claim settlement ratio (95%+), IRDAI regulation, a strong financial track record, and a wide range of life insurance plans. Shriram Life Insurance has a 98.52% claim settlement ratio, over 20 years of experience, and serves millions of policyholders across India — making it one of the most trusted life insurance companies in India.
